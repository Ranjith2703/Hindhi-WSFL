Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to learn Hindi?

With regular practice, most students achieve conversational fluency within 3–6 months. Exam preparation courses typically span 4–8 months depending on the level. Our personalized learning plans are designed to match your pace and goals.

Are online classes as effective as offline?

Absolutely. Our online classes use interactive video sessions, voice practice, and real-time feedback. Many students prefer online learning for its convenience. Both modes deliver the same quality curriculum and personalized attention.

Do you provide study materials?

Yes. All enrolled students receive comprehensive study materials including notes, vocabulary lists, practice exercises, and audio recordings. Materials are tailored to each course and updated regularly.

Is one-to-one coaching available?

Yes, we offer one-to-one conversation sessions as a core feature of our program. These personal sessions with the trainer focus on your specific weaknesses and accelerate progress significantly compared to group classes alone.

Do you prepare students for competitive examinations?

Yes, we specialize in exam preparation for NCERT, CBSE, Matric board, and Sabha examinations (Praveen, Visharad). Our targeted preparation approach has helped students consistently achieve excellent results in all major Hindi examinations.
